Subject: Rails security releases: 8.0.5.2 and 8.1.4.1

Rails Versions 8.0.5.2 and 8.1.4.1 have been released. These are **security
updates** addressing 21 security issues. We strongly recommend upgrading as
soon as possible.

Older versions of Rails are unsupported, and users are recommended to
upgrade to at least the 8.0 series. Security support for the 8.0 series
ends on November 7, 2026. See our [maintenance policy](
https://rubyonrails.org/maintenance) for details.
